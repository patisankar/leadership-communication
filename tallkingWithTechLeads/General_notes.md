Time Manangment Tip 
======
Starting the day early gives me dedicated time to organize my thoughts and plan effectively. I review any outstanding items from the previous day, assess urgent and important issues, and prioritize work using three to-do lists:
Today — immediate tasks that require focus and completion
Near-term — upcoming work that needs planning and sequencing
Long-term — strategic or less urgent items that require future attention

This approach helps maintain clarity, reduce reactive work, and stay focused on both short-term execution and long-term goals.

Team player - prespective
======
Maintain a clear distinction between personal and professional interactions by reframing conversations to remain respectful, objective, and outcome-focused. 
Prioritize facts over emotions to enable constructive collaboration, even in high-pressure situations.

**Apply a Five Whys mindset to teamwork and problem-solving:**

**Be curious**   diagnose before prescribing
**Be thorough**   avoid shallow fixes that cause regressions
**Read everything**   value context over speed
**Maintain objectivity**   separate facts from emotions, even under pressure
**Understand the big picture**   optimize for long-term system health, not local wins
