# Poor Sleep Day Checklist

## Morning
- [ ] Get 10–20 minutes of sunlight
- [ ] Drink water
- [ ] Delay caffeine 60–90 minutes
- [ ] Light movement (walk or stretch)

## Work Planning
- [ ] Prioritize low-risk / routine tasks
- [ ] Avoid major decisions or conflict
- [ ] Write things down / use checklists

## Caffeine & Energy
- [ ] Use small caffeine doses
- [ ] No caffeine after 2–3 PM
- [ ] Stay hydrated

## Midday
- [ ] Eat a protein-focused lunch
- [ ] Avoid heavy or sugary meals
- [ ] Optional 10–20 minute power nap (1–3 PM)

## Focus Management
- [ ] Work in short blocks (25–45 minutes)
- [ ] Take brief breaks
- [ ] Double-check important work

## Evening (Protect Recovery)
- [ ] Light dinner
- [ ] Dim lights after sunset
- [ ] Avoid screens 60–90 minutes before bed
- [ ] Go to bed slightly earlier

## Avoid
- [ ] Long naps
- [ ] Late caffeine
- [ ] Overworking
- [ ] Big decisions

## Goal
- [ ] Stay functional today without harming tonight’s sleep
