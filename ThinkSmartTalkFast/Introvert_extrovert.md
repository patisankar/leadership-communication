# How Introverts Can Act Like Extroverts in Meetings (Practical Playbook)

## Core Principle
You don’t need to change your personality. Focus on **observable behaviors**:
- Engagement
- Clarity
- Presence

---

## 1. Prepared Participation Model
Prepare before the meeting to reduce real-time pressure.

**Do:**
- Identify 2–3 points to contribute
- Prepare:
  - 1 opening line
  - 1 follow-up question

**Examples:**
- “I have a quick thought on scalability…”
- “One concern is around retry handling…”

---

## 2. Speak Early (Critical)
If you don’t speak in the first 5 minutes, it becomes harder later.

**Tactic:**
- Ask a simple clarification early

**Example:**
- “Just to confirm, is this impacting only the payments service?”

---

## 3. Use Structured Speaking
Avoid rambling—use a repeatable format:

**Formula:**
> Context → Point → Impact

**Example:**
- “From a performance perspective, this API might become a bottleneck. It could increase latency under peak load.”

---

## 4. Ask High-Impact Questions
Questions = low effort, high visibility.

**Types:**
- Clarifying → “What’s the expected QPS?”
- Risk-based → “What happens if this service fails?”
- Product → “How does this impact users?”

---

## 5. Use Micro-Interactions
Small signals show engagement without long speaking.

**Examples:**
- “Makes sense”
- “+1 to that”
- Nod + short acknowledgment

---

## 6. Build a Phrase Library
Predefined phrases reduce thinking effort.

**Examples:**
- “I agree with X, and one addition is…”
- “Another angle to consider…”
- “From my experience…”

---

## 7. Control Turn-Taking
Don’t wait for perfect timing.

**Entry phrases:**
- “Can I add something quickly?”
- “One quick point…”

---

## 8. Follow-Up After Meeting
Leverage async communication (introvert strength).

**Do:**
- Share missed ideas
- Highlight risks

**Example:**
- “One edge case is retry duplication—we may need idempotency keys.”

---

## 9. Manage Energy
Extrovert behavior consumes energy.

**Tips:**
- Pick key moments to speak
- Don’t try to dominate the meeting
- Take breaks between meetings

---

## 10. Mental Reframe
Instead of:
> “I need to act like an extrovert”

Think:
> “I need to make my thinking visible”

---

## Quick 30-Second Playbook
1. Speak once in first 5 minutes  
2. Add one structured point  
3. Ask one thoughtful question  
4. Follow up asynchronously
