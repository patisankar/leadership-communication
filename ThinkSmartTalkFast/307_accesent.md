# Speech-to-Text Speaking Practice

## Goal

Improve clarity and delivery while keeping your natural accent. Practice prosody: the stress, rhythm, pacing, volume, and intonation that help listeners follow your message.

## 5-Minute Practice

1. **Choose a familiar topic.** Speak for 30–60 seconds about a work update or another everyday subject.
2. **Record your first take.** On iPhone, open **Notes**, create a note, tap **Attachments → Record Audio**, and speak naturally.
3. **Review the transcript.** Tap the recording, then tap the transcription button. Transcription is available on iPhone 12 or later when the device language is supported, including English.
4. **Mark the script.**
   - Bold 3–4 important words in each sentence.
   - Add `/` where a short pause separates ideas.
   - Add `↓` at the end of statements and `↑` at the end of genuine questions.
5. **Record it again** using your cues. Listen to both versions and notice whether the key points are easier to follow and your sentence endings sound complete.

## Example

> Yesterday, I completed the snapshot ID change / and added the R1 credit-card test. ↓  
> Today, I’ll follow up with TJ / on the review. ↓

## What to Listen For

- Are the main points easy to understand?
- Did I emphasize the important words?
- Did I pause between complete ideas?
- Did I finish statements with a clear downward inflection?
- Did I slow slightly on the final words instead of trailing off?

Use the transcript to review wording and structure. Listen to the recording to assess pacing, emphasis, and inflection; the transcript cannot show those vocal cues.
