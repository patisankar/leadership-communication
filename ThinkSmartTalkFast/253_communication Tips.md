Question that we need to ask
==========
How to tailor communication to your audience

When preparing to write, teach, or present, ask four questions:

What is their knowledge level?
Adjust depth and terminology to meet them where they are.

What are their attitudes?
Are they supportive, neutral, skeptical, or resistant?

Where might they hesitate or push back?
Anticipate concerns, trade-offs, and risks before they are raised.

What motivates them—and how do they benefit?
Anchor your message in outcomes that matter to them.
