# Coding

## SMART Goal: Binary Search

Over the next **2 weeks**, solve **6 binary search problems**, spending **25 minutes per problem**.

Before coding, explain:

1. **Problem and constraints:** What am I finding, and would brute force be too slow?
2. **Why binary search:** What yes/no condition is monotonic?
3. **Search bounds:** What are the minimum and maximum possible answers?
4. **Correctness:** Why are my boundary updates and return value correct?
5. **Verification:** Which edge cases matter, and what are the time and space complexities?

**Success:** Solve at least **5 of 6 problems without hints**, explaining each decision.

**Busy-day minimum:** Spend **10 minutes** answering these questions for one problem.
